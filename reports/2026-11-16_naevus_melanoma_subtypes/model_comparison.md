# Model metrics across all experiments

Every model trained so far on the 1,000 beta images, from Sonnet's answers or from SkinGPT-R1, scored the same way on three tasks. Generated by stage S11 from S6 classify `670746415c57`, S10 ridge `159decea7f3d`, S13 bench `84d7b6fd7f04` and this stage; the numbers are in `model_comparison.csv`.

**Each model has one number.** A model is one combination of pipeline stage, input, classifier, training target and fold grouping. The same number in two tables is the same fitted model scored on a different task; a different number is a different model, even when the name looks alike (for example, the question ridge trained on two classes and the one trained on ten subtypes). The column *Trained on* gives the training target and *Score* how the task is read from the model's output. The model index at the end lists every number.

**How every number is computed.** Each image is scored by a model that did not see it in training (out-of-fold predictions; 5 repeats of 5-fold cross-validation). Within each repeat the predictions of all folds are pooled and the measure is computed once; the tables give the mean over the 5 repeats. Pooled values can differ by about 0.01 to 0.02 from the fold means quoted in earlier reports.

**What the measures mean.**
- **AUROC:** the chance that a randomly chosen positive case (malignant, melanoma) scores above a randomly chosen negative one, over all thresholds. 0.5 is chance, 1 is perfect.
- **Sensitivity:** of the positive cases, the share the model flags (P ≥ 0.5).
- **Specificity:** of the negative cases, the share it correctly leaves alone (P ≥ 0.5).
- **Accuracy:** the share of all images classified correctly (P ≥ 0.5). For benign against malignant there are more benign images (500 against 300), so accuracy leans toward specificity.
- **F1:** the harmonic mean of precision (the share of flagged cases that are truly positive) and sensitivity; high only when the model both finds the positives and does not over-call them.
- **Specificity at 95% sensitivity:** the share of negatives left alone when the threshold is lowered until 95% of positives are caught. The threshold is chosen on the same predictions, so this is slightly optimistic. It is the project's pre-set main score for benign against malignant.
- **Ten subtypes:** each image goes to its most probable subtype (no threshold). Sensitivity, specificity and F1 are computed per subtype and averaged. With 100 images per subtype, accuracy equals mean sensitivity (balanced accuracy).

The trained models output probabilities for equal class priors (balanced class weights, or the random forest and MLP corrected to equal priors), so P ≥ 0.5 means the same for each. The zero-shot SkinGPT-R1 diagnosis and the direct question are not calibrated that way, so their threshold metrics depend on where their scores happen to sit; compare them by AUROC. Bold marks the best value in each column of each table (ties included; not marked where more than half the rows share it).

Differences of 0.01 to 0.02 between models are within the spread over repeats and folds (about ±0.03 in AUROC for benign against malignant, ±0.04 for naevus against melanoma, where each class has only 100 images); the highest value in a column is not by itself evidence that the model is better.
