| 2026-11-14 | [How the ridge penalty is chosen, and how much it matters](2026-11-14_ridge_tuning/README.md) | New S8 tune stage: S6's design for ridge, reproduced exactly, with a 21-value penalty grid (lambda 1 to 100,000) scored inside and outside every outer fold. One broad optimum at lambda 20–100; the nested choice (lambda about 33 for the Directional Score) gives AUROC 0.840, 0.001 below the best value in hindsight. The finer grid changes nothing (0.839 with S6's grid); choosing by AUROC is no better and less stable than log loss; the one-standard-error rule costs up to 0.035.
