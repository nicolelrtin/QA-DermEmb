# Experiment reports

One folder per experiment batch, newest first. Each folder has a README with the purpose, the runs, the results and
links to the data. Every individual run also writes `outputs/qaemb/runs/<run_id>/report.md`
(indexed in [experiments_log.md](experiments_log.md)).

| Date | Report | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-11 | [Which questions the classifiers relied on](2026-11-11_question_importance/README.md) | New S7 importance stage on the S6 run. Ranking stable (models agree, lasso keeps 19 of the top 20 in ≥80% of fits) except between the two indeterminate subtypes. No single question beats asking directly (best alone AUROC 0.72, rough skin, vs 0.79); the gain comes from many weak questions. Sonnet answers yes to under 5% of images for 309 of 446 questions, so much of the signal is in how doubtful its "no" is, and an important question need not mean the feature was seen. Top questions mix lesion dermoscopy features with surrounding-skin ones (rough skin, sun damage, skin tone); the special-sites topic (63 questions) carries 2–6% of the importance |
| 2026-11-11 | [Classifiers on Sonnet's answers for the 1,000 beta images](2026-11-11_classifier_sonnet1000/README.md) | First S6 run on real answers (446 questions, 5×5 nested CV). Benign vs malignant: ridge on the Directional Score AUROC 0.84 and specificity 0.44 at 95% sensitivity, against 0.79 and 0.26 for Sonnet asked directly (higher in 23 and 25 of 25 folds). Directional Score beats P(yes) by about 0.02 AUROC for the logistic models; the Coherence Score adds nothing. Two-stage cascade worse than the flat 10-class model (0.41–0.45 vs 0.45–0.50) because stage 1 misroutes about 43% of images. Top questions include the skin around the lesion (sun damage, skin tone) |
| 2026-11-11 | [First runs through the Claude API: pilot, cost projection and the 1,000-image beta set](2026-11-11_api_pilot/README.md) | Sonnet answered all 501 questions (500 + the control question) on the 1,000 beta images through the Batch API: 42,000 prompts complete on the first attempt, $203.63 (total API spend $206.80 of $500); images sent at Sonnet's limit (3.6 MP); control question "Is this a malignant lesion?" in its own topic, never a feature, separates malignant from benign with AUROC 0.79 (sensitivity 56% / specificity 83% at P(yes) 0.5; specificity 22% at 95% sensitivity); 2-image pilot matched the earlier `claude -p` answers on 94.8% of questions |

Also: without negations, Directional and Coherence Scores correlate at r = 0.98 on the 2 proof-of-concept images |
