Correlation between every pair of questions

How far the groupings agree

Adjusted mutual information between two ways of grouping the questions: 0 is no more agreement than chance, 1 is the same grouping. Dotted lines: the 95th percentile with one grouping shuffled.

What the first ten components follow

Share of each component's variance explained by each variable (eta squared; r squared for age), shaded from 0 to 0.5. AUROC: the component alone as a score for malignant against benign (0.5 = none; below 0.5 points towards benign). Click a row for the questions that load most on it.
