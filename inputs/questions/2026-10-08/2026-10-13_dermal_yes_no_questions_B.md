# Dermal lesion yes/no questions (set B)


## Acquisition & modality

- **Y001** Is the image a dermoscopic (dermatoscopic) image rather than a naked-eye clinical photograph?
- **Y002** Is the image a close-up clinical photograph framed on a single lesion?
- **Y003** Is the image a regional overview photograph showing a body region rather than one lesion?
- **Y004** Does the image appear to be a lesion crop extracted from 3D total-body photography?
- **Y005** Does the image appear to have been captured with a smartphone camera?
- **Y006** Does the image show suppressed surface glare consistent with cross-polarized acquisition?
- **Y007** Does the image show signs of non-polarized contact dermoscopy, such as an immersion-fluid layer or contact-plate edge?
- **Y008** Is the dark circular edge of the dermoscope's field of view visible in the frame?
- **Y009** Is a ruler, scale bar or other size reference visible in the image?
- **Y010** Is a colour-calibration chart or reference patch visible in the image?
- **Y011** Is the background around the imaged body part plain and uncluttered?
- **Y012** Is the lesion approximately centred in the frame?
- **Y013** Is the entire lesion, including all of its border, contained within the frame?
- **Y014** Is an orientation marker (for example an arrow pointing toward the head) visible in the image?
- **Y015** Is the image a greyscale or single-channel image rather than a colour image?
- **Y016** Is the image a narrow-band or multispectral channel image rather than a standard RGB photograph?
- **Y017** Is the image a reflectance confocal microscopy (horizontal, cellular-resolution) section?
- **Y018** Is the image an OCT or line-field confocal OCT cross-section?
- **Y019** Is the image a high-frequency ultrasound scan of the lesion?
- **Y020** Is the image a SIAscopy-style parametric chromophore map rather than a colour photograph?
- **Y021** Does the image appear to be synthetically generated (GAN or diffusion) rather than photographed?
- **Y022** Does the image show signs of digital post-processing such as super-resolution, denoising or colour standardization?
- **Y023** Is the lesion lit by a direct on-camera flash that produces a hard reflection?
- **Y024** Is illumination uneven across the lesion, with one side noticeably darker than the other?
- **Y025** Does the image appear to be magnified roughly 10x or more relative to a standard close-up?
- **Y026** Is the skin surface viewed at a markedly oblique angle, causing perspective distortion of the lesion?
- **Y027** Does the image show the lesion under transillumination (light passing through tissue from the side)?
- **Y028** Is the image a non-centred skin-region crop containing several candidate lesions?
- **Y029** Is the image a still frame captured from video?
- **Y030** Is the lesion photographed under ultraviolet or Wood's lamp illumination?
- **Y031** Is diascopy (glass pressed on the lesion to blanch it) being performed in the image?
- **Y032** Is a smartphone dermoscope adapter visible in the frame?
- **Y033** Is the skin being stretched or pinched by fingers during capture?
- **Y034** Is the lesion lit by coloured (non-white) light?
- **Y035** Do some structures appear in only one of a paired polarized and non-polarized image?

## Image quality & artefacts

- **Y036** Is the lesion in sharp focus across most of its area?
- **Y037** Is motion blur visible in the image?
- **Y038** Is the resolution high enough to resolve individual dots a fraction of a millimetre across?
- **Y039** Are hairs lying over the lesion?
- **Y040** Do overlying hairs obscure more than about a quarter of the lesion area?
- **Y041** Are air bubbles visible in the dermoscopy immersion fluid?
- **Y042** Are surgical-pen or skin-marker ink marks visible on or near the lesion?
- **Y043** Is an adhesive dot, sticker or label visible on the skin?
- **Y044** Are blown-out (overexposed) highlights present within the lesion?
- **Y045** Is the image underexposed so that structures in dark regions of the lesion are lost?
- **Y046** Does the image have an obvious colour cast (for example strongly yellow or blue white balance)?
- **Y047** Are JPEG compression blocks or colour banding visible?
- **Y048** Is the lesion partly hidden by clothing, jewellery, a glove or fingers?
- **Y049** Is a tattoo visible within or immediately adjacent to the lesion?
- **Y050** Are smears or streaks of dermoscopy gel obscuring structures?
- **Y051** Is visible makeup, cream or sunscreen residue present on the skin?
- **Y052** Does the skin look wet or sweaty, adding extra specular reflection?
- **Y053** Is lesion-to-skin contrast so low that the lesion edge is hard to locate?
- **Y054** Is blanching from dermoscope contact pressure visible, potentially hiding vessels?
- **Y055** Would a reader likely judge the image unevaluable for diagnosis?
- **Y056** Is a biopsy site, scar or suture from a previous procedure visible on the lesion?
- **Y057** Are text annotations, watermarks or timestamps burned into the image?
- **Y058** Is the image a composite or collage of several views?
- **Y059** Are de-identification masks (blurred or blacked-out regions) visible in the image?
- **Y060** Are vertical or horizontal sensor-noise streaks visible across the image?
- **Y061** Is chromatic aberration (coloured fringes at high-contrast edges) visible?
- **Y062** Is there visible digital zoom pixelation around the lesion?
- **Y063** Is depth of field so shallow that part of a raised lesion is out of focus?
- **Y064** Are reflections from the dermoscope's LED ring visible?
- **Y065** Is high-ISO sensor noise (grain) visible?
- **Y066** Is the lesion near an image corner where lens distortion is greatest?
- **Y067** Is the lesion fewer than about 100 pixels across in the image?
- **Y068** Is the image mirrored or flipped (for example reversed text)?
- **Y069** Are unmasked patient identifiers (face, identifying tattoo, name tag) visible?
